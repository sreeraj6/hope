CCTV and security planning for shops, offices, and commercial premises in Vallikkavu.

For retail stores, offices, supermarkets, and other commercial spaces.

A business system needs to reflect how the premises operate. Entrances, billing areas, stockrooms, and shared workspaces all have different requirements. We discuss these priorities together with recording access, installation timing, and network needs.

A plan that fits your space.

  • Coverage planning for key business zones
  • Recording and access requirements
  • Coordination with network infrastructure
  • Installation scope and future expansion planning

Before we begin.

Share your operating hours, approximate floor layout, number of zones, and who will need access. Any customer-facing or workplace recording should be planned with privacy and appropriate notice in mind.

Expansion depends on the recorder, network, cabling, and available capacity. Mention future plans when discussing the initial setup.
