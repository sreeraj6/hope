Privacy policy

How this website handles your enquiry and contact information.

Who we are

This website represents Hope Networking Solutions, based in Vallikkavu, Kerala, India. For privacy questions, contact +91 95676 50963.

Your enquiry

The form asks for your name, phone number, service, property type, location, and requirements. Additional project details are optional. The website prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser and does not send the form to a website database.

You can review the message before opening WhatsApp. Selecting “Open WhatsApp” places the message in a WhatsApp URL and hands those details to WhatsApp. You still need to press Send there. WhatsApp’s own privacy practices apply, and your browser or WhatsApp may retain that URL or message.

Storage and retention

The website does not intentionally save form values in cookies or browser storage. Values stay in the current page while you edit or review them. Use “Clear enquiry” to remove them, or leave the page. Your browser’s own autofill features may behave separately.

If you send the message, Hope Networking receives it through WhatsApp and uses it to respond and discuss your requirements. Contact us to ask about retention or request deletion of enquiry records; any necessary business records may need to be retained.

Analytics and hosting

This release includes no third-party analytics, advertising trackers, embedded social feeds, or map embeds. The hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information, such as IP addresses, for service operation.

External links

Phone, WhatsApp, and Instagram links open the corresponding service. Their privacy practices apply when you use them. Avoid including passwords, camera access credentials, or other sensitive information in your enquiry.

Changes

This notice should be updated if the website’s data handling changes. For questions, call the contact number above.
