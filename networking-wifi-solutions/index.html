+ HELPFUL TO KNOW

Your questions, answered.

01Will a new router fix every coverage problem?+

Not necessarily. Layout, wall materials, placement, and interference matter. A combination of access points and wired connections may be more appropriate.

02Can CCTV use the same network infrastructure?+

Compatible systems can share planned infrastructure. Network capacity and access requirements should be considered together before installation.

03Do you provide an internet connection?+

This service covers local network planning and installation. An internet subscription and provider availability are separate.
