+ HELPFUL TO KNOW

Your questions, answered.

01Can you plan for an existing home?+

Yes. Existing routes, power points, and finishing constraints can be discussed when assessing the installation.

02Does CCTV prevent every incident?+

No system can guarantee that. Cameras can support visibility and recording, alongside sensible physical security and access practices.

03Do I need internet for recording?+

Some local recording systems can record without internet. Remote access generally needs a suitable connection. The exact behavior depends on the chosen equipment.
